The app

  • Nothing leaves your device. Halotone sends no data to me or anyone else. It has no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • Songs and settings are saved on your device only. iOS may include them in your device backup to iCloud or a computer, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.
  • Halotone Plus is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; I never see your name, Apple Account, or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether Plus is unlocked.
  • Crash reports. If you chose in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send me crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. You can turn this off under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.
  • Beta testing. If you test Halotone through TestFlight, Apple shares the feedback you send with me, together with the details Apple names in TestFlight.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes; see GitHub's privacy statement. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or external fonts.

Email

If you write to me, I use your address and message only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). The mailbox is hosted by IONOS SE in Germany. I delete messages once they are no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to, correction, deletion, or restriction of your data, object to its processing, and ask for it in a portable format. You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Bavarian authority (BayLDA). Since Halotone collects nothing, I usually hold no data about you; for anything else, write to me.
